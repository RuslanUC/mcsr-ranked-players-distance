Yes.

You can specify any two players that played at least one ranked match in seasons 1-11, and there will be a match chain between them.

Right now, matches from seasons 1-11 are scanned, so if player you are trying to look up hasn't played in these seasons, they are completely unknown for this website.

Also, not all latest games from season 11 are scanned right now. If player has played their first game ever after 28.07.2026, it may not be scanned yet.

One more thing, only ranked matches are scanned, so private rooms don't count.

If you are sure that player has played at least one ranked game since the start of season 1 until 28.07.2026, please let me know.

If error does not say that player is unknown - that's very strange, please let me know nicknames of players you are trying to look up.
